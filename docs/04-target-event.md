**Caution:** the jury names visible in the site bundle belong to the

Format vs the previous (Jan 2023, remote) edition: in-person, fixed A2J
theme instead of free challenge choice, university course integration,
and a split build week → pitch day → award ceremony structure — meaning
**ten days of build time between the hackathon week and the pitch**.

## Not the same event: Munich Hacking Legal

The other Munich legaltech hackathon (April, TUM) is run by different
organizers (START Munich + Liquid Legal Institute + Legal Tech Colab,
with Stanford CodeX cooperation). MHL 2026 recap, useful as a
calibration reference:

- 24h sprint, Apr 25–26, 2026, teams ≤4, challenges revealed at start.
- Main sponsor Siemens; challenge sponsors Siemens, BMW Group, ELTEMATE,
  EQS Group. Supporting: Lovable, Google Cloud, OpenAI (credits), Otto
  Schmidt (legal DB).
- Overall winner: team **Fidevus** (3 students, law+tech mix) solving
  ELTEMATE's "AI-supported data breach response assistant" challenge;
  prize included entry to Cambridge's Hack the Law. Both years' winners
  were interdisciplinary teams. (Winning stack not publicly reported.)
- Jury of 8 incl. sponsor representatives; no public judging rubric.
- A typical non-winning stack from participant posts: multi-agent
  orchestration + legal-DB-grounded retrieval with citations + a
  document-verification layer flagging risky language — i.e. the
  citation/trust pattern was already table stakes in the room.
