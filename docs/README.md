# Research & preparation docs

Working documents behind this repo: multi-agent web research on the
legaltech hackathon landscape, a gap analysis of what legal AI still
cannot do, 15 scored project scenarios, and a domain-law primer.
Research snapshot: **July 2026**. Every substantive claim carries its
source; statistics were verified at research time but age quickly.

## How these were produced

Multi-agent research workflows (parallel web-research agents with
structured-output schemas, source URLs required per claim), followed by
a five-lens scenario generation pass grounded in the gap findings, and
a judge-panel scoring pass. Scenario ideas are therefore anchored to
cited evidence rather than free brainstorming — each scenario's
`why_gap` field points back to specific findings in doc 05.
